# Simple Interest Calculator

A lightweight, user-friendly tool to quickly compute simple interest, total repayment amounts, and breakdown schedules based on principal, annual interest rate, and time duration.

---

## Features

- **Quick Calculations:** Compute total interest earned or owed in seconds.
- **Flexible Timeframes:** Calculate interest across years, months, or days.
- **Detailed Summary:** View principal, calculated interest, and final maturity amount side by side.
- **Input Validation:** Prevents negative values, invalid characters, and zero-duration inputs.

---

## Formula Used

Simple Interest is calculated using the standard formula:

$$I = P \times r \times t$$

Where:
- **$I$** = Total Interest
- **$P$** = Principal Amount (initial investment or loan)
- **$r$** = Annual Interest Rate (as a decimal, e.g., $5\% = 0.05$)
- **$t$** = Time duration (in years)

Total Amount ($A$) payable or earned:

$$A = P + I$$
